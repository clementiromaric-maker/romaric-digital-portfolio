RESEARCH AND POSITIONING BASIS - V1.8.0

The refinement was benchmarked against four current themes.

1. STRATEGIC PROCUREMENT
CIPS distinguishes strategic sourcing and category management from transactional purchasing through longer-term value, structured sourcing decisions, supply-risk analysis and supplier relationship choices.
Relevant sources reviewed:
- CIPS, Differences With Strategic Sourcing
- CIPS, Strategic Sourcing
- CIPS, Category Management
- CIPS, Kraljic Matrix

2. FUTURE-READY PROCUREMENT
Deloitte's 2025 Global CPO Survey emphasises core procurement knowledge, digital readiness, technology that augments work, and the combination of human talent with digital capabilities.
Relevant source reviewed:
- Deloitte, 2025 Global Chief Procurement Officer Survey

3. FUTURE SKILLS
The World Economic Forum's Future of Jobs Report 2025 highlights analytical thinking, technology literacy, resilience, leadership and social influence, systems thinking, curiosity and lifelong learning.
Relevant source reviewed:
- World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs Report 2025

4. STRONG PORTFOLIO CASE STUDIES
Professional case-study guidance consistently recommends making context, personal contribution, decisions, process and outcomes easy to find, especially for time-limited hiring-manager review.
Relevant sources reviewed:
- Interaction Design Foundation, case-study structure and hiring-manager review guidance
- Adobe, case-study guidance on selecting proof and showing results

APPLICATION TO THIS PORTFOLIO
- Results are surfaced earlier.
- Personal role and authority boundaries are explicit.
- Strategic growth is shown as a natural extension of documented operational work.
- Digital and AI content remains subordinate to procurement judgement and operational value.
- The portfolio does not use generic innovation claims where verified examples can be shown instead.
