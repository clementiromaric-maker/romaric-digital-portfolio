RC DIGITAL PORTFOLIO V6.0 - EXTERNAL BENCHMARK
Date: 11 Aug 2026

Sources checked before the rewrite
1. Interaction Design Foundation portfolio and case-study guidance
   - Recruiters/hiring managers may decide within a few minutes whether a portfolio is worth a screening call.
   - The opening should quickly answer who the candidate is, what they can do and how they work.
   - Keep the best relevant cases prominent and side projects supporting.
   - Case studies should show context, decisions, process and outcomes without unnecessary description.

2. Hays Denmark career guidance
   - Clear hierarchy, concise language, readable presentation and concrete outcomes are stronger than jargon-heavy descriptions.
   - Plain language should not be sacrificed for technical terminology.

3. Current portfolio examples
   - Nicholos Palmer: clear Web Producer / Web Content identity, immediate production/CMS/QA proof and concrete case studies.
   - Dominic James: challenge, role, implementation, constraints/outcome, with AI described as support rather than identity.
   - Creative Bloq 2026 portfolio examples: clean, project-focused portfolios are effective when the work remains the main attraction.

4. Practitioner/forum signal
   - Current experienced UX discussion explicitly identifies both too much process and too little process as failure modes.
   - Treat forum evidence as anecdotal, but it supports the broader benchmark: show enough process to prove judgement, not so much that the portfolio becomes a methodology presentation.

5. Current technical/security sources
   - Next.js July 2026 security release: 16.2.11 is the Active LTS patch addressing the published July security issues.
   - Next.js App Router docs: route groups support multiple root layouts; static export remains supported through output: 'export'.
   - Cloudflare Workers static assets docs: public/_headers is supported for custom security headers.

Decision taken
- Keep structure where the work is genuinely sequential.
- Remove repeated self-branded frameworks from personal philosophy.
- Put live work before supporting pilots.
- Use an established role-market identity instead of a self-invented title.
- Keep one concise professional boundary and one central AI disclosure.
